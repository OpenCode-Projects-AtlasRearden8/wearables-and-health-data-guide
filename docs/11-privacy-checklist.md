# The Privacy Checklist

Run this once per season (or whenever you change devices, accounts, or households). Fifteen minutes, a pencil, and each item is pass/fail — no scoring anxiety survives this page, and every "fail" is simply next-month's single knob from [the monthly ritual](09-the-monthly-data-ritual.md).

Date of audit: ____________    Season: ____________

## A. The own-ecosystem basics

- [ ] **A1. My wearable connects to my phone's health store** (Apple Health / Health Connect / maker store) as I intend it to — and *only* in the direction I intend.
- [ ] **A2. I know where the maker's cloud fits**: my history syncs there, so a lost watch doesn't erase history. I've chosen this trade consciously.
- [ ] **A3. My maker account has a strong, unique password and two-factor authentication** where offered.
- [ ] **A4. I could find my data-export path in under two minutes** if someone asked me today. (If not, reread [the export chapter](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md) until you can.)

## B. Other people

- [ ] **B1. Every person in my activity-sharing circle is someone I chose, this month, on purpose.** Ghost followers removed.
- [ ] **B2. Every family-health feature I've enabled is known to the person wearing the device** — no silent switches, either direction ([family chapter](06-family-sharing-and-kids-accounts.md) guardrail).
- [ ] **B3. Emergency/SOS contacts are current** — the people the device would call are still the people it should.
- [ ] **B4. No one else's health data is visible through my logins** (partner dashboards, kids' pages) except with their deliberate yes.

## C. Location

- [ ] **C1. Workout GPS recording is on or off because I decided** — and I know where routes are stored ([export chapter](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md)).
- [ ] **C2. My health apps' phone location permission is "While Using" (or justified "Always")**, not whatever they asked for on install day.

## D. Third parties and data flows

- [ ] **D1. Every connected third-party app is one I used this month**; stale grants revoked.
- [ ] **D2. I have never uploaded a full health export to an AI site, free analyzer, or random cloud tool** — and the anonymized-only rule is on paper near my computer ([export chapter](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md)).
- [ ] **D3. Optional sensors I don't want collected are turned off at the source** (background SpO2, temperature sampling, etc.) — the strongest privacy setting is a stream that doesn't exist.
- [ ] **D4. Ad personalization / "insights sharing" toggles in the account screens are OFF.**

## E. Household-sensitive checks (pause here if any apply)

- [ ] **E1. No child's device gamifies weight, calories, or body-fat estimates.** If one does, that's today's single knob. ([family chapter](06-family-sharing-and-kids-accounts.md))
- [ ] **E2. Kids' location sharing is purpose-based** (school-run safety style), known to the kid, and off outside its purpose.
- [ ] **E3. Teens have the account we agreed on** — their own login where age-appropriate, sharing offered *by them*.
- [ ] **E4. After any relationship change or move**, sharing settings were reset deliberately (separations, roommates, new caregivers).

## F. Closing

- [ ] **F1. I could pause every sharing setting from the wearer's own device** if I wanted to disappear from somebody's dashboard today.
- [ ] **F2. Next quarterly audit is in the calendar.**

---

**Scoring, honestly:** count fails. Zero fails = done for the season. One to three fails = pick *one* as this month's single knob in the [ritual](09-the-monthly-data-ritual.md); the rest roll to next season. Anything you circled but can't fix in-app (an app with no opt-out, a maker with no export) is real **comparison-shopping knowledge** for your next device — write the brand's name in the margin and let it inform the future purchase, quietly.

Fails you deliberately leave unfixed are allowed. This checklist is for living with tech kindly, not for winning something.
