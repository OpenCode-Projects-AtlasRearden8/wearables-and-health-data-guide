# Glossary

Plain-English definitions, in the spirit of the whole book: no condescension, no hand-waving. Words cross-referenced to the chapter where they're explained in context.

**Actigraphy** — Reading sleep by watching movement. The old, honest core of sleep tracking: still for a while = probably asleep. Your wearable blends it with pulse data. See the [honesty chapter](03-what-the-numbers-honestly-mean.md).

**Always-on display** — A watch face that never sleeps. The biggest battery cost at the display level; see [battery habits](08-battery-habits.md).

**Apple Health / Health Connect** — The phone's health "file drawer": where your health apps file readings on your own device, and where the umbrella export lives. See [exporting](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md).

**Auto-pause** — A workout feature that stops timing when you stand still (at lights, tying shoes). It occasionally pauses your run at a traffic light or mid-tie, which is allowed.

**Baseline** — *Your* normal, measured over weeks. The only reference number a wearable score should ever be compared to: never another person's. Central to the [monthly ritual](09-the-monthly-data-ritual.md).

**Battery anchor** — A fixed daily time-slot (in a shower, at breakfast) for charging, so the tracker is worn when it matters. See [battery habits](08-battery-habits.md).

**CSV** — "Comma-separated values": the plain spreadsheet format that opens anywhere, stays readable for decades, and is the friendliest export in health data. See [exporting](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md).

**Cycle tracking** — Tracking menstrual cycles via temperature trends, timing, and logged events. Extremely sensitive data; handled with the strictest defaults in the [privacy chapter](07-privacy-settings-worth-touching.md).

**Data-decode worksheet** — This book's printable one-page monthly summary: [chapter 10](10-data-decode-worksheet.md).

**Export** — Getting a copy of your data *out* of an app or service, onto your own storage, in a format that outlives the service. See [chapter 4](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md).

**FIT / GPX / TCX** — Specialized files for workout routes and track data. Notation you can mostly ignore until you change ecosystems.

**Health store** — Collective nickname for Apple Health and Health Connect (the phone-side file drawers). See above.

**Heart-rate variability (HRV)** — The tiny millisecond flutter between heartbeats. Real physiology; *your own baseline* is its only meaningful reference; drives the "recovery/readiness/stress" score family. See the [honesty chapter's](03-what-the-numbers-honestly-mean.md) derived-scores section.

**Heart-rate zones** — Heart-rate bands used to pace workouts, auto-calculated from age formulas unless you tune them. Estimates with elbows; see [setup](02-setting-up-your-watch-kindly.md).

**JSONL / logbook file** — One JSON record per line: the repairable, boring storage format used by the companion `vitals-logbook-mcp-server` for home-measured readings. One tool per job: see the [welcome chapter](01-welcome.md).

**Light / deep / REM sleep** — Real sleep stages, measured in a lab with brain electrodes. On a wrist: a *shape estimate*, not a chart. Treat stage minutes as decoration; treat sleep *timing* as signal. See the [honesty chapter](03-what-the-numbers-honestly-mean.md).

**Notification mirroring** — Showing your phone's notifications on your wrist. Also the most privacy-leaky default in wearable land; trim it deliberately (see [setup](02-setting-up-your-watch-kindly.md) and [privacy](07-privacy-settings-worth-touching.md)).

**Patient portal** — Your clinic's secure website/app for messages, records, and document uploads. The best channel for your one-page trend summary. See [sharing with care teams](05-sharing-trends-with-your-care-team.md).

**PPG (photoplethysmography)** — The optical heart-rate sensor's method: green light into skin, watch how much blood absorbs per pulse. Explains exactly why wrist heart rate has its error bars.

**Resting heart rate** — Your heart rate when calm and still, most reliably during sleep. The most trustworthy trend line most wearables draw on a consistent wearer; the recommended centerpiece of the [monthly ritual](09-the-monthly-data-ritual.md).

**Readiness / recovery / body-battery scores** — Proprietary composites (mostly HRV + sleep + motion) offered as a "how ready is your body today" number. Snapshots of labor, not verdicts on your body; never compare across people.

**Sleep debt / sleep score** — Proprietary numbers grading your sleep against a target. Consistency nudgers, not report cards.

**SpO2** — Blood-oxygen saturation spot-checks from the watch. Not a clinical pulse oximeter; never a basis for action on its own. See the [honesty chapter](03-what-the-numbers-honestly-mean.md).

**Streak** — A run of consecutive goal days. Motivational double-edged sword; a gentle goal you *can* keep beats an impressive one you can't (see [setup](02-setting-up-your-watch-kindly.md)).

**Theater mode** — A watch mode that silences and blacks the screen (movies, mowing). Doubles as battery savings in [battery habits](08-battery-habits.md).

**Trend** — A direction across weeks. The most useful sentence in this book: *a single reading is a sketch; a trend is a story.*

**Two-factor authentication (2FA)** — A second proof (code, app tap) beyond your password. Turn it on wherever it's offered; thirty seconds of cost per login for real protection.

**Umbrella export** — The "Export All Health Data" move at the phone-health-store level: the broadest, best-insurance export most people can run. Twice a year; see [chapter 4](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md).

**vitals-logbook-mcp-server** — The companion local tool from Asdale Dynamics, LLC that keeps home-measured readings (cuff, thermometer, weight) in a JSONL logbook with trends and CSV export. This book handles the wearable side; it handles the deliberate side. See the [welcome chapter](01-welcome.md).

**Wrist detection** — A feature that locks the watch when it's off your wrist and often enables better measurement mode. Also stops nudging while charging.
