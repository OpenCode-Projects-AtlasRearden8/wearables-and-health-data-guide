# The Data-Decode Worksheet

Print this page (the site's Print button at the top gives you a clean sheet), fill it in during your [monthly data ritual](09-the-monthly-data-ritual.md), and keep the pages in a folder or shoebox. One worksheet = one month = one story. Ten of them make a year you can hand to a clinician or read yourself with real satisfaction.

> **Education only, as everywhere in this manual:** this worksheet records *what you observed*. It never renders *what it means for your body* — that interpretation belongs to you and your care team. "Unexplained change" gets actioned by [bringing it to a professional](05-sharing-trends-with-your-care-team.md), nothing else.

---

## Part 1 — The header (fill in once)

```text
Month: ______________________          Worksheet date: ______________

Question for this month (one only):
_______________________________________________________________

Wearing device (brand/model): ___________________________________
Wearing nights out of 30 (honest): /30
Battery habit this month (anchored / chaotic): ______________
```

## Part 2 — The three looks (from the app's month views)

```text
1. Resting heart rate
   Usual band this month: ________ to ________
   Notably high days (dates): _______________________________
   Notably low days (dates): ________________________________
   Direction vs last month (up / down / steady): ____________

2. Sleep timing (lights-out / wake)
   Usual lights-out: ____________    usual wake: ____________
   Later-than-usual nights (dates/why, if known): ____________________
   Naps this month (rough count): ____
   Direction vs last month: ____________

3. Movement (steps or active minutes)
   Usual day: ____________   best week: ____________
   Lowest week + any obvious cause: ______________________________
   Direction vs last month: ____________
```

*If you also keep home readings with the `vitals-logbook-mcp-server` companion:*

```text
4. Home logbook readings (optional line)
   Readings tracked this month: ______________________________
   Anything the notebook flagged that the watch can't see: ____
```

## Part 3 — The reading (what changed, what didn't)

```text
STABLE (things I can count on):
  - ___________________________________________________________
  - ___________________________________________________________

CHANGED (with dates):
  - ___________________________________________________________
  - ___________________________________________________________

UNEXPLAINED change lasting 2+ weeks:
  - What: ______________________________________________________
  - Since (date): ______________
  - Action decided (circle one):
      [ mention to care team at next visit ]
      [ prepare a one-page summary NOW (ch. 5) ]
      [ other, written by me: ______________________ ]
```

## Part 4 — The one knob

```text
This month I changed (tool, max one):
  [ ] a notification/goal setting        -> ch. 2
  [ ] a battery anchor                   -> ch. 8
  [ ] a sharing/privacy setting          -> ch. 6, 7
  [ ] an export/backup task              -> ch. 4
  [ ] nothing — everything stays as-is

Notes for future me (one or two lines):
  _____________________________________________________________
  _____________________________________________________________
```

## Part 5 — The closing sentence

Circle, then read aloud:

> **"I looked, I noticed, I made one change (or zero), and I don't need to look again until next month."**

---

## Part 6 — Dated extras (once a quarter only)

On the third month of each quarter, replace Part 4 with these two quick forms from their chapters:

- **The umbrella export** ([ch. 4](04-exporting-your-data-safely.md)): exported on ______, filed to ____________________, verified openable: [ ] yes
- **The privacy checklist** ([ch. 11](11-privacy-checklist.md)): pass count ____ / total, dated ______

## A filled sample (fictional, for shape only)

> *Month: October. Question: "Is sleep getting calmer since I moved the screens out of the bedroom?" Resting HR: usual band steady — no notable days. Sleep timing: lights-out ~10:40 most nights except Oct 14–18 (a work crunch, ~1 a.m.) — recovery by Oct 20. Movement: usual day unchanged; lowest week Oct 14–18 same crunch. Unexplained 2+ weeks: none. One knob: none — screens move worked, staying as-is. Closing sentence: said, out loud, standing up, kettle on.*

That's the whole ritual in one page. Notice what the sample *doesn't* contain: no verdicts, no scores, no stage minutes, no comparisons with other humans. Dates, directions, one question, one answer's worth of action. A clinician reading twelve of these pages a year later has far more than any single screenshot could ever give — and so do you.
